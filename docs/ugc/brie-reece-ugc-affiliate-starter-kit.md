## GetYourGuide (live commission path)

Brie is already signed up for GetYourGuide. First check: Partner / Creator account vs regular booking account. Only the Partner Portal pays commission.

A regular GetYourGuide account at getyourguide.com is for booking your own trips. It does not pay commission. A Partner / Creator account lives on partner.getyourguide.com (the URL must include "partner"). Official onboarding warns that logging into the customer site without "partner" in the URL means bookings will not generate commissions.

### If you already have Partner / Creator access

1. Open the Partner Portal and log in at the Partner Dashboard (partner in the URL). Use Affiliate Link Builder: paste any GetYourGuide experience URL → Create short URL → copy your gyg.me/... link.
2. Promote trip-matched tours: Morocco / Marrakech (medina food walks, Agafay desert sunsets), city walks, boat days, Arctic-style winter trips, food tours. Put gyg.me links in IG bio, Reels captions, Stories, and X.
3. Track payouts. Bookings report shows Completed / Pending / Canceled. Commission is paid only after the traveler attends. Canceled bookings pay nothing.

### If you only have a regular booking account

1. Apply as a partner / content creator at https://partner.getyourguide.com/en-us/signup/ — choose Content Creators, add Instagram / TikTok / YouTube, activate via email, then log into the creator / partner portal.
2. Optional: after partner signup, complete the Community application in Opportunities (review usually 4–7 business days) for sponsored-experience promos.

### How commissions work (official docs)

- Base commission starts at 8% on bookings through partner links / integrations (Partner onboarding + Creator Affiliate guide).
- Creator guide: purchases within 30 days of clicking your link are attributed to you; you can earn up to 8%.
- Payouts: calculated on completed bookings; Partner onboarding says payments process on the 5th of each month; Creator guide lists bank transfer or PayPal. Bank-transfer threshold noted as $50 USD in partner onboarding.
- No commission on canceled bookings; pending until the travel date passes.

## FTC disclosure

If you are paid, gifted, or earn affiliate commission, you must clearly disclose that material connection. Use #ad or a clear “affiliate / paid partnership” line people can notice without digging. This is not legal advice — it is the standard disclosure expectation for endorsements.

- Put the disclosure at the start of the caption (and on-screen for video when possible), not buried after hashtags.
- Amazon Associates, marketplace UGC fees, gifted product, and GetYourGuide commissions all need disclosure.
- Official FTC endorsement pages were blocked/404 from the fetch tools used for this kit; when you post, open the current FTC Endorsement Guides / influencer disclosure materials on ftc.gov yourself and follow the latest wording.
